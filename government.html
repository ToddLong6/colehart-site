Government & Prime Contractors

Technical capability, qualified personnel, and program support for government requirements.

For contracting officers, prime contractor business-development teams, and program managers. ColeHart supports requirements through prime contracting, subcontracting, and defined project delivery.

ColeHart Enterprises LLC is a veteran-owned small business providing technology and IT support, program and project support, data and analytics, technical staffing, and technical documentation for government agencies and the prime contractors that serve them.

Core Capabilities
Prime Contracting
Direct award delivery for defined technology, program-support, documentation, and staffing scopes within ColeHart's capability.
Subcontracting
Federal and state subcontract support to primes — defined technical work packages, surge capacity, and qualified personnel.
