Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · ColeHart Enterprises LLC

1. Scope

This policy describes how ColeHart Enterprises LLC ("ColeHart," "we," "us") handles information collected through this website, colehartenterprises.com. It does not govern information exchanged under a signed contract, teaming agreement, or statement of work, which is handled according to those agreements.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the information you submit through our contact and inquiry forms — such as your name, email address, phone number, organization, inquiry type, and the details of your message.

We also collect standard web analytics data (pages visited, session duration, approximate location, browser and device type) through Google Analytics to understand how our site is used.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, evaluate potential engagements or partnerships, and follow up about services you asked about. We use analytics data to improve the content and performance of the site.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Form submissions are processed by our form and email delivery providers (currently Formspree and EmailJS) solely to route your message to us and send you a confirmation. Analytics data is processed by Google. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Data Retention

Inquiry records are retained for as long as needed to respond and maintain a record of the conversation, and then deleted or archived. You may request deletion of your inquiry data at any time by contacting us.

6. Security

This site is served over HTTPS/TLS. While no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, we take reasonable measures to protect the information submitted through the site.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data you submitted through this site. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@colehartenterprises.com.
