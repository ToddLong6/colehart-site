Who We Are

About ColeHart Enterprises LLC

ColeHart Enterprises is a veteran-owned technology and professional services company supporting government agencies, prime contractors, and commercial organizations.

We provide technology expertise, data and program support, and qualified technical personnel to help our clients execute critical projects and meet operational requirements.

Our Background

ColeHart was built on a foundation of military service, operations leadership, project coordination, and hands-on technology experience.

That background shapes how we work: clear scope, documented deliverables, and follow-through. Whether the engagement is an IT project, a reporting build-out, a documentation effort, or a contract technical resource, our goal is dependable execution.

What We Do
  • Technology Services — IT infrastructure, systems, networks, cloud, Microsoft 365, automation, documentation
  • Data & Program Services — analytics, dashboards, reporting, business analysis, program coordination
  • Workforce Solutions — qualified technical and professional personnel for project, contract, and subcontract needs
How We Deliver

We work through prime contracting, federal and state subcontracting, prime-contractor partnerships, commercial B2B projects, and contract staffing.

Engagements are structured as project-based work, staff augmentation, subcontract support, or managed deliverables — matched to how each client contracts.
